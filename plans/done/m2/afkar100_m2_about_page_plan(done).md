## Content Mapping

- **The Vision (Mission Statement):** Clear explanation of the "$100 limit" and the focus on Arab entrepreneurs.
- **How it Works (The Competition Cycle):** Visual/text breakdown of the weekly flow (submit, vote, win).

- **Trust & Transparency:** Brief notes on how ideas are reviewed and the fairness of the competition.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Blocks:**
    - "Submit your idea" for entrepreneurs.
    - "Sponsor a day" for businesses.
    - "Vote" for the community.
