# Afkar100 M3 Plan: Sponsor Management

**Source:** `plans/afkar100_project_plan.md`  
**Scope:** Milestone 3, Sponsor Management only  
**Milestone Goal:** The admin can manage sponsor records and track prize responsibility.

---

## Milestone

Deliver sponsor management tools for creating, editing, activating, and tracking sponsor-related obligations.

---

## Tasks

- [ ] Add a new sponsor with name, logo, assigned day, and contract dates
- [ ] Edit an existing sponsor
- [ ] Activate or deactivate a sponsor
- [ ] Track weekly prize payment status for each sponsor

---

## Deliverables

- [ ] Sponsor create form
- [ ] Sponsor edit form
- [ ] Sponsor activation controls
- [ ] Sponsor payment tracking view

---

## Done Criteria

- [ ] Admin can create a sponsor record
- [ ] Admin can edit sponsor details
- [ ] Admin can activate or deactivate a sponsor
- [ ] Prize payment status is visible per sponsor
